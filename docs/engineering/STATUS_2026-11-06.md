## Speakable text and the template linter (N10)

- Question audio is synthesised from a *speakable* form of the shown text: equipment codes are
  read the way people say them (`IDF-2` is "I D F 2", in Hindi "आई डी एफ़ टू"; `C2-7` is
  "C 2 7", never "C twenty-seven"; `GATEWAY` and `MAIN` are words). The screen keeps the text
  as printed on the equipment. Hindi place names are now Devanagari (Hinglish keeps the Latin
  ones), so Hindi questions no longer switch script mid-sentence.
- The linter renders every probe at every place, with and without the walking prefix, in all
  four languages, plus the consent line, the confirmation prefix and the presence questions, and
  checks: every language present, placeholders, script, no raw codes left for speech, length
  (25 words, 35 with the prefix), no instruction to operate equipment, no named cause, and that a
  question offers answers the extractor can read.
- It found two real problems, both fixed with a failing test first: the Hindi and Hinglish socket
  question named no answer the extractor knew, and "charge hone lagta hai" ("it starts
  charging", the natural answer) was read as the hedge "lagta hai" ("it seems").

## Presence checks and disagreement explanations (N06)

- **S7.** A decisive answer about a restricted place someone walked to is checked by asking them
  to read a label only someone there can see. The simulator draws the value per scenario and
  alone checks readings; the core never learns it. Through the API with a real simulator: an
  honest electrician passes and S1 stays correct; a lying electrician fails and the decision is
  still correct; a guard claiming the closet fails and the engine abstains rather than decide on
  his word. Off by default (`presence_checks`), a toggle on the create screen.
- **S2.** When two people disagree, the engine reports P(both right), who is likelier mistaken,
  and the component only one of them depends on. In S2 (AP 2A failed, rooms 205 and 210): "Both
  can be right if the cause is: Access point 2A failed (93%). Only Neha's place (room 210)
  depends on Access point 2A; room 205 is also covered by Access point 2B."
- The console's old contradiction panel only compared answers about the same thing in the same
  place, so S2 never appeared there. The new Testimony panel shows both.

## Load test (D21)

`scripts/load_test.py` runs a real simulator and core as processes on their own database, with
Gnani emulated at the network level (`scripts/gnani_emulator.py`), and drives the three voice
bots unchanged: each clip's audio carries its answer, so answers cross the real call socket,
recognizer session, second decode, extractor and engine.

Ten minutes: 45 investigations back to back, all verified, correct on reveal and replay
byte-identical; 0 call socket errors; at most 3 recognizer sessions at once (cap 16), none left
open. Core memory 111 MB at the start, 141 MB peak, 133 MB at the end (mean 130 MB in the first
half, 137 MB in the second): not shown to be flat; a longer run is needed to tell warm-up and
bounded caches from a leak. A one-minute trial before it found the race fixed below.

## Human-like and junk voice runs (no volunteers available)

`scripts/voice_bot_run.py` can now answer like people: a random take per answer from the
open-source MMS voices (other phrasings, phone-band and noisy versions; `--variants`), a pause
before speaking (`--think-s`), irrelevant speech first (`--junk`: real off-topic Hinglish and
Marathi speech, "पता नहीं", noise) or silence until the re-prompt (`--silent-p`). Every recorded
answer is scored against what was said (`--report`, kept private).

- Every junk utterance in an off-topic and phone-talk set was also run through every question
  in every language (2,072 extractions). It found three ways junk became evidence, now fixed
  and tested (`services/core/tests/test_extraction_junk.py`): phone talk ("haan haan bolo",
  "haan, hello") read as yes, being-here talk ("haan, main yahin hoon") read as yes, and an
  answer about another thing ("corridor ki lights on hain" for the CCTV screen). The labelled
  extraction sets score the same as before.
- Live on Gnani, three human-like runs and six junk runs (S1, three calls at once): no junk
  became evidence in any run, silences were re-prompted, every run that decided was correct
  and verified, and every replay was identical. With junk on half of all answers one run
  abstained instead of deciding, which is the intended behaviour when answers run out.
- Synthetic voices do not replace M2: real accents, rooms and phrasing still need people.
- Gap noted, not fixed: if a question that asked someone to walk is withdrawn while they walk,
  the engine still plans them at their old place (it never learns they moved).
