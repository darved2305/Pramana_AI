# Stopping-rule calibration (2026-11-05)

Goal from the handoff: make the planner abstain more where abstaining is the safe answer
(unidentifiable scenarios, 68% in `benchmark-2026-11-05`) without losing accuracy.

## Method

Variants were explored on **tuning seeds 0-599** only, then the candidates were run once on the
reported seeds 10000-10299 (this directory, generated by
`python -m pramana_eval --n 300 --seed-start 10000 --policies E,E-stricter,E-ask_more`). The
earlier report (`benchmark-2026-11-05`) is unchanged.

Tuning seeds (0-599), our planner E:

| Variant | Success | Accuracy, identifiable | Accuracy, decided | Abstention recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| base (tau_lo 0.80) | 80.3% | 71.4% | 90.7% | 67.4% |
| tau_lo 0.75 | 83.2% | 76.1% | 90.6% | 65.9% |
| tau_lo 0.85 | 76.7% | 64.7% | 91.8% | 73.2% |
| tau_lo 0.90 | 69.2% | 51.5% | 93.8% | 81.9% |
| effort valued at half (lambda 1/120) | 83.3% | 75.1% | 92.7% | 66.7% |
| abstain when causes are indistinguishable from everywhere reachable (0.05 to 0.20) | identical to base | | | |

## Findings

1. **Abstention cannot be raised without losing accuracy by moving the threshold.** In the
   unidentifiable tuning scenarios where E decides anyway it is right 42 of 45 times: the
   alternatives nobody present can rule out are low-prior fault pairs. A stricter threshold
   also stops identifiable investigations, whose confidence at that point looks the same.
   On the reported seeds `E-stricter` (tau_lo 0.85) abstains 1.7 points more where it should
   and loses 3.7 points of success (paired CI [+1.3, +6.0] in favour of E).
2. **An identifiability-aware rule had no effect**: the probability on causes that are
   indistinguishable from the leading decision at every reachable site never reached 0.05 at
   the moment of a moderate decision. It was not kept.
3. **Asking a little more when unsure helps.** Valuing a person's time at half
   (`E-ask_more`, lambda 1/120 min per s) improves success by 2.7 points on the reported seeds
   (paired CI [+1.0, +4.7]), decided accuracy 94.8% vs 92.9%, identifiable accuracy 82.9% vs
   79.0%, at +0.37 questions and +15 s of effort per investigation; abstention recall is
   66.1% vs 67.9% (one scenario) and false confidence 2.0% vs 1.7%.

## Decision

Not changed yet: the default exchange rate stays 1/60. Switching to `E-ask_more` is a product
choice (more questions for more accuracy) and changes S1's question path, so it needs the team
and a re-run of the M1 and voice checks. Recommended: adopt it after the demo recording, or
before if the team prefers accuracy over the shortest S1. The claim "abstains more without
losing accuracy" is **not supported** and must not be made.
