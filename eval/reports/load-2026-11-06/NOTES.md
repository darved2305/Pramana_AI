# Load test, 2026-11-06: three calls at once for ten minutes (emulated Gnani)

Generated by `uv run python scripts/load_test.py --minutes 10 --out eval/reports/load-2026-11-06`.
`report.md` and `summary.json` are the generated output; this note says what they do and do not show.

**What was real.** A simulator and a core as separate processes, PostgreSQL (a dedicated
`pramana_load` database, recreated for the run), fresh keys, the participant call sockets, one
recognizer session per question, the second decode of decisive answers, the extractor, the
engine with three questions in flight, approval, capability, repair, verification, reveal and
replay. The three voice bots of `scripts/voice_bot_run.py` ran unchanged.

**What was emulated.** Gnani (`scripts/gnani_emulator.py`): its wire formats only. A clip's
audio carries its answer text, which the emulator returns as the transcript. Nothing about
Gnani's accuracy, latency or limits is measured, and no timings are reported.

**Supported by this run.**
- 45 investigations back to back (each S1: CB_C2_7 tripped), every one verified, correct on
  reveal and byte-identical on replay; no call socket errors; 311 voice answers.
- At most 3 recognizer sessions at once, all closed at the end: sessions do not leak, and the
  governor cap (16) was never approached.
- Questions withdrawn mid-call (5) ended their turns without breaking the call.

**Not supported.**
- That memory is flat. The core's working set went from 111 MB to a peak of 141 MB (mean 130 MB
  in the first half, 137 MB in the second). Ten minutes cannot tell warm-up and bounded caches
  (engine per investigation, question audio) from a slow leak; a longer run is needed.
- Anything about real speech: noise, accents, silence timers and barge-in with people.
- Question audio was synthesised only 9 times: the same questions repeat, and the core's
  in-memory audio cache served the rest, as it would in production.

**Code.** The run used commit `c288652` plus the fix it found (an answer racing the
conclusion was refused with `INVESTIGATION_NOT_ACTIVE` instead of "question withdrawn",
which made a call fail; seen in a one-minute trial before this run). The fix and this script
are in the commit that adds this note.
