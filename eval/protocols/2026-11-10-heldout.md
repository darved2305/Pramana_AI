## Scope

Run the logic-mode generator on the campus block C pack for all 300 seeds. Use identical seeded
scenarios for the sequential E planner, checklist, random selection, greedy entropy, and E with
three-way parallel scheduling. Do not call an LLM, Gnani, or another provider. Do not change
engine, policy, scenario, metric, analysis, or generator code after this protocol is committed.

## Metrics and analysis

- Keep historical success for RC1 comparability and report honest success separately. Honest
  success is a correct decision or an abstention only when scenario identifiability is false.
  Pair identifiability is unknown, so pair abstentions do not count as honest success.
- Report pair first useful action and full pair resolution separately. Full resolution requires
  the simulated repair, verification, reopen and follow-up diagnosis loop to clear both faults
  and reach engine verification. Include pair denominators, repair counts, and extra verification
  probes.
- Report decided accuracy, accuracy on identifiable scenarios, false confidence, abstention
  recall and precision, calibration bins, and false-confidence and abstention counts by scenario
  stratum. Include the generated scenario manifest.
- Evaluate success and decided accuracy for E, checklist, random and greedy entropy at question
  budgets 1 through 10 and on a shared effort grid in 60-second increments. Use paired scenarios
  and 95% scenario-bootstrap intervals. Generate deterministic SVG plots and a reliability
  diagram with bin counts.
- Preserve bootstrap 95% intervals with 10,000 paired resamples. Use exact two-sided McNemar for
  honest success, Wilcoxon signed-rank for probes and effort, and Holm correction separately for
  each outcome across the registered five comparisons: E vs A, B, C, D, and E with K=1 vs K=3.
  Keep an unavailable comparison in the family as p=1 for adjustment and print its p-value as
  unavailable. Report denominators.
- Exclude failed policy episodes from outcome rates and report their count. Paired analyses use
  only seeds completed by both policies. Budget intervals use 2,000 scenario resamples.

## Seed audit and deviations

The B1 brief requested seeds 20000-20299 and asserted they had never been used. That assertion is
false: `eval/tests/test_benchmark.py` uses seed `20_000` in the presence test and seeds
`20_000-20_029` in its reproducibility check; `docs/EVALUATION.md` also documents the separate
presence experiment starting at 20000. RC1 seeds 10000-10299 were likewise examined before the
freeze. Neither range can be called held out.

Before freezing this protocol, a repository, evaluation-report, and lane-report/log search found
no prior run or test using 40000-40299. The only matches were this planned seed reference; no
benchmark report or invocation on the range existed. Seeds 40000-40299 are therefore used as the
disjoint replacement range.

- Deviation: the requested 20000-20299 range was already used, so this protocol uses the searched,
  disjoint range 40000-40299 instead.
- Deviation: this run has 300 randomized scenarios, matching the B1 lane's requested 300-seed
  range, below the broader evaluation document target of at least 500 randomized scenarios plus
  curated cases. The smaller sample limits precision and is reported without extrapolation.
- Deviation: policy B is not run because no Groq or Gemini API credential is configured and paid
  providers are prohibited. Its primary-family slot remains unavailable and is retained for Holm
  adjustment.
